# Dalangtan Playa, Qaidam Basin (Qinghai, China)

This cell is the top one in the Jezero ranking outside the known analogs (0.84); its neighbor, 46350, is second [1]. It contains the Dalangtan salt playa, in the northwest of the Qaidam Basin: the literature places it between 38°0′ and 38°40′ N and between 91°10′ and 92°10′ E [2].

**What is known.** It is not a discovery: it has been studied as a possible Mars analog since 2008, for its magnesium sulfates, chlorides and perchlorates [2]. Its hypersaline deposits, arid climate and high ultraviolet radiation make it an analog for its geomorphology and for the preservation of life [3]. Under the rocks there are microbial communities that could serve as a refuge [4].

**What it could be used for (hypothesis).** Rehearsing the search for biosignatures in evaporites and the detection of perchlorates before taking the instruments to Mars.

**Where the analogy breaks down.** Mean precipitation from 1980 to 2011 was about 50 mm per year and the mean annual temperature was 3.5 °C [2]. Our minimum temperature is −19 °C, versus −80 °C at Jezero [1]. The low pressure and the carbon dioxide atmosphere of Mars are not replicated [1]. The 1,361 NCBI records partly reflect how much it has been studied [1].

**What to check in the field.** Whether the playa's perchlorates and sulfates are also present underground, and how much they depend on air humidity.
